About Us

Some ideas want to be explained. Others would much rather be played.

At RippleGames, we look for the game hiding inside a subject, a story, an event, or a problem. Sometimes it becomes a board game. Sometimes it lives on a screen. Sometimes it takes over a room and gets people moving, talking, competing, or conspiring.

We start with what you want people to experience and build from there.

Because not every idea needs another presentation. Some are better when you step inside them.
